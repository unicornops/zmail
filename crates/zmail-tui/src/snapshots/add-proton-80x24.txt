  A third-party client for Proton
  Zmail is an independent client, not made or supported by Proton. It talks to
  Proton's private API the way Proton Mail Bridge does and signs in as Bridge,
  because Proton doesn't accept other clients, so your Proton sessions list
  shows it as Proton Mail Bridge. Proton may change or block it at any time, and
  only allows it on paid plans. Zmail keeps a session token and the key that
  unlocks your mail in the system keychain, never your password.
