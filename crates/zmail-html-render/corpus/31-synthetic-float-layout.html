Letters from the Allotment

A monthly note on growing things

[photo]

October is the month of putting things away. The beans are done, the squash are curing on the shed roof, and the last of the tomatoes are ripening on the kitchen windowsill. It is also, perhaps surprisingly, a month of planting: garlic goes in now, and so do the broad beans that will give us the first green of next spring.

This year we tried a new mulch of shredded leaves over the asparagus bed. The worms have been busy.

A reader asked about saving seed from peppers. The short answer: pick the best fruit from the healthiest plant, let it over-ripen, scrape the seeds onto a plate and dry them somewhere warm and airy for a week.
